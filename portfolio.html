NHS Waiting List Analytics (opens on GitHub)

GitHub

Tests whether changes in NHS waiting lists come from treatment capacity or from the number of patients starting treatment pathways, using NHS England Referral to Treatment statistics.

  • Waiting list rose from about 4.4 million before COVID to about 7.5 million in 2024 to 2025
  • Waits over 52 weeks grew from under 2,000 to over 300,000
  • Python, MySQL, Databricks and Power BI used end to end
